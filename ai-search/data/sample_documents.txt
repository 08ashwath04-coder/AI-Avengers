SECOND BRAIN AI - COLLEGE KNOWLEDGE BASE

ATTENDANCE POLICY

Students are required to maintain a minimum of 75 percent attendance.

Students whose attendance is below the required percentage may not be eligible to appear for semester examinations according to college rules.

LIBRARY POLICY

Students can borrow library books for a maximum period of 14 days.

A student can renew a library book if there is no reservation from another student.

Students should return library books before the due date.

EXAMINATION POLICY

The semester examination timetable will be published by the academic department.

Students must carry their college identity card during examinations.

Students should report to the examination hall before the scheduled examination time.

IDENTITY CARD POLICY

Students must carry their college identity card while inside the campus.

The identity card may be required when entering examination halls, laboratories and other restricted areas.

LABORATORY POLICY

Students must follow laboratory safety instructions.

Students should not use laboratory equipment without permission from the instructor.

Students must keep the laboratory workspace clean after completing practical work.

ACADEMIC SUPPORT

Students can contact department faculty members for academic guidance.

The college may provide additional academic support sessions before semester examinations.
